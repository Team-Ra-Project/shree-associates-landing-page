Frequently Asked Questions

Society registration is the formal process of registering a housing society, credit society, seva society, trust or mandal with the applicable government authority so that it can function as a recognised legal entity.

Housing society committees, managing committee members, society members and new or developing housing societies can get in touch for guidance on registration and related documentation.

A conveyance deed is a legal document that transfers ownership rights of the property from the builder to the housing society.

Deemed conveyance is a process that allows a housing society to obtain ownership rights of the property when the builder has not executed the conveyance deed within the expected timeframe.

Conveyance helps establish clear property ownership records for the society, which supports proper documentation and reduces administrative complexity related to the property.

The specific documents depend on the society's situation and the service required. During the discussion and document review stage, the available papers are examined and the required set is clarified.
